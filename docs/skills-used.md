## Impeccable detector results

- `npx impeccable detect app components lib`: **0 findings** (`[]`).
- URL scan of the running app at 390×844 and 1440×900 (`CI=1 CHROME_PATH=…`). First run, two findings:
  - `flat-type-hierarchy` (warning): sizes were 15/17/20 px. **Fixed**: wordmark to 24 px, idle heading to 34 px, list label to body size.
  - `repeating-stripes-gradient` (advisory). **Ignored on purpose**, in `.impeccable/config.json`. The stripes are the kerb hatching on the jam strip. They encode where a chokepoint falls on the route and whether it is at peak, and they mirror the painted yellow-and-black kerbs at Bengaluru junctions. That is information, not surface decoration.
- Re-scan at both viewports: **0 findings**.

## Critique pass (Impeccable `critique` + Emil review), what it changed

Found by looking at real screenshots (mobile, desktop, dark), then fixed:

| Before                                                             | After                                                                                               | Why                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Top pick's reason: "13 min slower, with no clear upside"           | Reason names the weighted factor that won ("Skips ORR near Ecospace, which can flood… Adds 5 min.") | A reason that contradicts the ranking breaks trust.                |
| Top pick's steps open on load                                      | Highlighted, steps closed until tapped                                                              | Steps took half the sheet.                                         |
| Two tied routes both labelled "Fastest"; tie reason "0 min slower" | One "Fastest", one "Same time", honest tie copy                                                     | Labels must be unambiguous.                                        |
| Loud green "Find routes", disabled when empty                      | Hidden until both places are set; outline style                                                     | Search runs automatically; the primary action is choosing a route. |
| Every chokepoint in the city on the map                            | Only ones the shown routes touch                                                                    | Clutter hid the routes.                                            |
| Map blank                                                          | Worker shipped; container sized; SVG sketch fallback                                                | Real bugs (see decisions.md).                                      |

## Loomcraft UX/UI checklist, second review

- Contrast AA: computed, see the table in decisions.md. Lighthouse accessibility 100.
- Focus, hover, active on every control: yes. Focus ring 3 px in signboard green; slider thumbs have their own ring.
- Breakpoints 320/768/1024/1440: layout switches at 900 px. Mobile and desktop screenshots checked. 320 px relies on truncation in the search summary; not screenshotted.
- Semantic tokens: yes (`--paper`, `--ink`, `--signboard`, `--kerb`, `--flood`, `--brick`).
- No `!important` except the reduced-motion override, which needs it to beat component transitions.
- Mixed styling: Tailwind utilities plus one `globals.css` for tokens and stateful components (jam strip, sheet, sliders). A deliberate split, noted here.
